# SphereServer 0.55 Series Changelog Summary

> Major architectural expansion introducing modular scripting, dynamic events, custom gump dialogs, and filesystem manipulation.

---

## Key Milestone Features

- **`[EVENTS]` System**: Allowed attaching arbitrary collections of event triggers to characters and items dynamically.
- **`[TYPEDEF]` System**: Separated item types from hardcoded IDs, enabling scripted item mechanics.
- **`[DIALOG]` Engine**: Introduced custom gump packet composition with resizepics, dhtmlgump, buttons, and radio controls.
- **`FILE` Scripting Object**: Enabled reading, writing, and creating text files directly from scripts.
- **Advanced Math & Bitwise**: Added `<muldiv>`, `<eval>`, and `<qval>` ternary operators.
